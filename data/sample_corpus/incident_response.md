# Incident Response Runbook

## Severity levels

A severity 1 incident is a full production outage or confirmed data breach. A severity 2 incident is a partial outage affecting more than 10 percent of customers. A severity 3 incident is a minor defect with a workaround.

## Response targets

Severity 1 incidents must be acknowledged within 15 minutes and updated on the status page every 30 minutes. Severity 2 incidents must be acknowledged within 1 hour. Severity 3 incidents are handled during business hours.

## Post-incident review

A blameless post-incident review must be held within 5 business days of resolving any severity 1 or severity 2 incident. The review produces a written report with a timeline, root cause and follow-up actions. Follow-up actions are tracked in the engineering backlog with named owners.
