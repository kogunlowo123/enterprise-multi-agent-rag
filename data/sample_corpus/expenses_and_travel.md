# Expenses and Travel Policy

## Expense reimbursement

Employees must submit expense reports within 30 days of the purchase date. Receipts are required for any expense above 25 dollars. Reimbursements are paid in the next scheduled payroll cycle after manager approval.

## Business travel

Flights must be booked at least 14 days in advance in economy class. Hotel stays are capped at 220 dollars per night in major cities. Rental cars require approval from a director and must be the compact or mid-size category.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. A home office stipend of 500 dollars is available once every 2 years.
