# Information Security Policy

## Password requirements

All employee passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords must be rotated every 90 days for privileged accounts. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for every system that stores customer data.

## Data classification

Data is classified into four tiers: public, internal, confidential and restricted. Restricted data must be encrypted at rest with AES-256 and encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher. Access to restricted data requires written approval from the data owner.

## Device management

Company laptops must use full-disk encryption. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the security team within 4 hours. Personal devices may not store restricted data.
